About

Pefon (meaning “good language”) is a minimalist constructed language created in three days by LJG of the Binomial Cisplatin team. It is the simplest, easiest to learn and most complete language.

Pefon has only 58 words, written in both a pictographic and a Latin script. It is an analytic language with free word order; the main orders are SVO and SOV. All of its basic grammar takes 7 minutes to learn.

Scale

Pefon was released on 31 July 2026. As of 4 November, it has 438 learners and users.

On 14 October, Pefon overtook Toki Pona to become the second-largest constructed language in China, counted by the size of China’s largest community.
